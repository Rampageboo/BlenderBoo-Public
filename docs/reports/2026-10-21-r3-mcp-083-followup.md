# BlenderBoo 0.8.3 R3 and MCP follow-up (2026-10-21)

The Owner supplied `2026-10-21-r3-r6-asset-replay.md` and clarified that the
delivered Flashlight is a test case, not an asset to modify. This change
targets toolkit behavior. No production `.blend`, FBX, or GLB was saved.

## Repairs

- **MCP result semantics:** default responses no longer turn a semantic
  `changed:false` result into `{"ok":true,"changed":false}`. Small results
  retain `status` and `reason`. Large `mesh.fair_fit` analysis returns
  `status`, coverage, hit counts, coefficients and residual p95 inline; the
  fitted-radius and residual grids remain retrievable through a response
  artifact. The short text channel also shows the refusal or analysis summary
  when a client does not display structured content.
- **Lathe caps:** a circular rim now maps to a quad grid with four deliberate
  rim corners per cap. A 32-segment synthetic lathe measured 320 quads, eight
  valence-3 cap corners, zero slivers, zero self-intersections and 1.0 loop
  continuity; `asset.quality_gate` passed. Evidence:
  `2026-10-21-r3-grid-cap-quality.json`.
- **Projected plates:** thickness grows away from the target. Before creating
  an object, the tool searches a bounded, minimum outward offset until its
  triangulated shell no longer intersects the target; unresolved penetration
  returns `status:unsupported`. In the sphere fixture, 76 overlap pairs became
  zero. All 384 signed-distance samples were outside, with minimum clearance
  0.318 mm at scene scale 1. Evidence: `2026-10-21-r3-plate-clearance.json`.
- **Shading:** constructors set smooth face shading and mark explicitly sharp
  rings plus dihedral edges over 35° sharp, keeping rounded surfaces smooth
  and hard transitions crisp.
- **Radial comparison:** when no explicit `radial_max_mm` is supplied, the
  region-display threshold is the p90 paired absolute radial delta. The
  threshold is reported as a display choice, not as a pass/fail criterion.

## Scope and remaining limits

The Flashlight delivery remains as measured in the supplied replay: 124
triangle faces and real part crossings. `export.game_asset` was reported to
round-trip on the Helmet; this follow-up did not repeat that production run.
`mesh.clear_of` remains a rigid-part lift, and `mesh.fair_fit` remains a
nonpolar fit that refuses incomplete rays. R2 still measures fidelity to the
AI surface, not whether a low mesh is artistically fair; choosing a fairness
threshold requires separate calibration against the rejected versions.

## Verification and deployment

Focused pre-install checks passed 82/82 unit tests, including an SDK-visible
default-profile R3 refusal and fair-fit analysis, plus R3/R2 Blender headless
fixtures. All 72 installed add-on source/data files matched by SHA-256. A fresh
Blender 5.2.2 process loaded installed 0.8.3, and its configured editable MCP
adapter also reported 0.8.3. `system.doctor` was healthy; schema audit found
184 tools, zero errors and zero warnings. Real MCP calls showed the lathe
refusal reason and fair-fit analysis, built a lathe that passed
`asset.quality_gate`, and constructed a projected plate. No production scene
was saved. Installed receipts are `2026-10-21-blenderboo-083-addon-smoke.json`
and `2026-10-21-blenderboo-083-adapter-smoke.json`.
