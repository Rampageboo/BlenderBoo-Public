# v0.6.4 validation

Date: 2026-09-25. Target: Blender 5.2.2 LTS on Windows. Tests used isolated
Blender processes and test scenes; the Owner's open document was not changed.

## Results

- `assembly.analyze` passed scaled 0.3 m / 30 m contact and intersection
  fixtures, including a coplanar pin head, flush tab, sub-tolerance 0.05 mm
  embed, 2 mm overlap, tube-end overlap, and an open shell. An open target
  reports undetermined penetration. A roughly 200,000-triangle pair completed
  in 1.239 s in the integrated headless run. Penetration remains sampled
  evidence, not an exact volumetric solution.
- Camera calibration recovered synthetic perspective (fixed and free focal)
  and orthographic cameras from exact/noisy correspondences and rejected
  degenerate inputs. The updated headless run also passed stale-state and
  reference/output-path checks. Contour comparison measured identical and
  displaced synthetic silhouettes and returned a review image/focus data.
  These are synthetic qualifications, not acceptance against Owner references.
- Document-local Python modules passed put/import/update/reimport/remove and
  save/reopen checks. Exec receipts and adapter call logs carry source hashes.
  Retained source versions grow within the `.blend`; call-log redaction and
  code-size limits mean a log alone may not contain every byte needed to replay.
- Fixed-name checkpoint save, explicit overwrite, and asset-keyed retention
  passed headless checks. Background render rejected invalid options before
  returning an operation ID; string-array vector options were normalized.
